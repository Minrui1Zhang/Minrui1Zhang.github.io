About

INTRODUCTION

I am a master's student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), in the Department of Electronic Engineering and Information Science, and a member of the Cross-Media Intelligence Group (USTC-CMI).

My primary interest is LLM Agents — especially how agents perform multi-step reasoning, long-horizon planning, memory management, and learning from feedback. Previously, I worked on retrieval-augmented generation in my undergraduate thesis.
